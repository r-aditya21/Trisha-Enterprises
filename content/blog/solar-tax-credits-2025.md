## The 30% Investment Tax Credit

The federal ITC allows homeowners to deduct 30% of installation costs from federal taxes. SolarPulse provides documentation and guidance throughout the process.

## State and local incentives

Many states offer additional rebates. Our team identifies every applicable program during your free consultation.

## Timeline matters

Systems must be placed in service within tax year guidelines. Contact us early to maximize credits for your install season.
