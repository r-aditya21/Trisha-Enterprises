## Why add storage?

Batteries store excess solar production for evening use and grid outages. Pairing storage with solar can increase self-consumption above 80%.

## Popular options

We install Tesla Powerwall and Enphase IQ Battery systems, integrated with our AI monitoring platform.

## Sizing your battery

Capacity depends on critical loads and outage duration goals. Our engineers model your usage patterns during site assessment.
